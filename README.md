<h2>Features</h2>
<p>1. Detects 4 diseases by imaging the eye state, Cataract, Anemia, Pinkeye and Jaundice.</p>
<p>2. Low to no cost digital diagnostic tool, only requires having a device with a camera and web browser to be used</p>
<p>3. Easy Technical project with High Impact and can be improved by research (regarding accuracy & reliability)</p>
